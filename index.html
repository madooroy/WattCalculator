ⓘ About IF & TSS

Intensity Factor (IF) and Training Stress Score (TSS) are calculated from average power, not Normalized Power (NP). That's accurate for steady efforts like trainer sessions or time trials; for rides with lots of surges, TSS will read low.

Calories assume about 1 kJ of work ≈ 1 kcal burned, since the body is roughly 20–25% efficient.
